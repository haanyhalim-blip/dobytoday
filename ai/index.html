Use your lists with Claude or ChatGPT

Connect your AI once, and it can read and change your to-do list, packing list and shopping list for you – just by asking.

Things you can ask

  • “Put everything from this email on my to-do list for tomorrow.”
  • “Add ‘pay the football club’ to Today and mark it urgent.”
  • “Make a packing list for 5 days in Rhodes with a 12-year-old, hand luggage only.”
  • “Turn this recipe into a shopping list for 6 people.”
  • “What's left on the house project? Add an update to step 3: rang the plumber.”
  • “Tick off milk and eggs.”

Before you start

Sign in on DobyToday, PackbyBag or ListbyAisle (top right, Sign in – just your email, no password). Your AI works with the lists saved in your account.

Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Handy Little Lists and paste this address:
    https://nlbeqaoffratriysazrx.supabase.co/functions/v1/lists
  3. Press Add, then Connect. Sign in with your email code and press Allow.
  4. In a chat, make sure Handy Little Lists is switched on (the tools menu under the message box), then ask away.

ChatGPT

  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps & Connectors, press Create. Name it Handy Little Lists, paste the same address, and choose OAuth for authentication:
    https://nlbeqaoffratriysazrx.supabase.co/functions/v1/lists
  3. Sign in with your email code and press Allow. Then choose Handy Little Lists in a chat and ask away.

Menus in ChatGPT change from time to time – look for “connectors” or “apps” in Settings.

Good to know

  • It can only reach your own lists – not your email, and nobody else's lists.
  • Changes show on the sites within about 20 seconds while they're open, or as soon as you open them.
  • Shared lists and projects update for everyone you've shared them with.
  • To disconnect, remove it from the Connectors page in Claude or ChatGPT.
  • Free to use. You use your own Claude or ChatGPT account; adding your own connectors may need a paid plan on theirs.
