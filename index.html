Ready-made days & common questions
  
Ready-made days

  
  • Airbnb changeover – Guests out, guests in. The whole turnaround in order, from stripping the beds to the check-in message.
  • School day – The morning scramble and the evening reset, so tomorrow morning is calmer than today's.
  • Sunday reset – An hour or two that makes the whole week easier. Nothing heroic – just the basics done.
  • Working from home – A work day at the kitchen table, with the house jobs slotted around it instead of eating it.
  • Guests for dinner – Friends coming round tonight. Everything done before the doorbell, not during.
  • First day back from holiday – Suitcases in the hall, empty fridge, 400 emails. The order that gets you back to normal by tonight.

  
Questions

  
How does Do by Today work?

  
Type your jobs for today, or start from a ready-made day. Each job lands under morning, afternoon, evening, anytime today or if there's time – so you can see what has to happen when, and tap jobs off as you go.

  
What happens to jobs I don't finish?

  
Done jobs disappear overnight, so tomorrow starts clean. If anything was left undone, you choose: keep it on today's list or let it go.

  
Can the whole house share one list?

  
Yes. Share a link and everyone works off the same list, ticked off as jobs get done. Or send it as a message by WhatsApp or email.

  
Is it free?

  
Yes – free, with no account and no sign-up. Your list stays on your phone unless you share it.
