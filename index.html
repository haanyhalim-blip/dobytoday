Why DobyToday?

  
A to-do list for today only, sorted by when – morning, afternoon, evening, if there's time. Tap things off as you go. Tomorrow it starts clean, so the list never becomes the thing you're avoiding.

  
Free, no account. Share the link and the whole house works off the same list.
